# The KamdenAI Signal System

The current member experience centers on an opening board with up to eight ranked candidates. Each candidate has its own decision. There can be zero, one, or several confirmed signals, up to eight.

## Status comes before rank

| Member label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Confirmed | An official signal for that session, with its own entry, stop, and target |
| Watch only | Observation and study; not an official entry |
| No buy | Completed review did not qualify the candidate; later price movement does not rewrite that decision |
| Ungraded | Evidence is incomplete; no official entry or return is assigned |

For example, rank one may be Watch only while ranks two and five are Confirmed. Only those two confirmations belong in the official signal list. Do not turn a study candidate into a signal because it later gained.

## A session in practice

1. Open Feed â†’ Signals and select today's session. Check the actual Published time and connection notice. Waiting placeholders and cached records do not establish a fresh signal.
2. Read each status. Preparation and research before the opening publication are context only.
3. Review the posted entry, stop, and target against your broker's current pricing and your own limits. A reference price is not a streaming or guaranteed executable quote.
4. Consider combined exposure across all positions. Saved sizing settings illustrate shares and risk; they do not reserve cash or place protective orders.
5. Record your actual execution in Journal. You may skip any or all signals.
6. Review the 10:05 results, including losses, no fills, and unresolved evidence.

Opening publication follows the opening review and may arrive later than its reference time. The actual dated publication controls interpretation; reopening an old session does not create a new alert.

## Morning outcomes

The current result model uses the published signal entry and dated post-publication price evidence to establish eligible execution before the morning exit window closes. No eligible entry touch means No fill. Incomplete evidence remains unavailable.

After modeled entry, the published target or stop can close the position. If neither does, the remaining modeled position uses the 10:05 AM Eastern reference exit. Read each record's execution basis and included cost assumptions. Confirmation alone does not prove a fill, and these records are not your broker P/L.

## The separate 3 PM study

The afternoon study compares the same morning names later. For current multiple-confirmation sessions, confirmed-name holding comparisons hypothetically hold from a post-publication reference entry to the 3 PM reference price, ignoring earlier target and stop exits. Watch-only movement assumes no entry or holding. Missing evidence remains unavailable.

These comparisons are excluded from official morning returns. Older sessions can have different policies; preserve their original labels and assumptions rather than applying today's model to them.
